# Code of Conduct

Nano Origin is for people who want to build, test, and discuss a browser in good faith. Treat contributors, users, and maintainers with basic respect.

Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, and repeated bad-faith disruption are not welcome. Critique code, decisions, and claims—not people.

If a discussion gets heated, pause it. Maintainers may edit, lock, or remove content that makes the project harder for others to take part in.

Report a concern privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be handled as fairly and discreetly as possible.
